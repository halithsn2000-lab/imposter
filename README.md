 # Imposter
 a game made for fun game nights with friends and family without having to pay anything or watch 20 ads

## How to use:
- 1 enter the players names
- 2 select a category
- 3 choose your game settings (time , hints , plottwist!)
- 4 start the game , when the timer starts running the group goes one by one and every body says something about the word and crew tryes to figure out who the imposters are while they try to blend in
- 5 voting : vote and see who won

  ## Features:
- 1 100% free to use
- 2 no ads
- 3 10 fun categories to choose from
- 4 no download requried
- 5 you can use it in most languages (using the built in browser translation) because it is web based

  ## Why i built this:
    when i meet up with family we usually play imposter but the available games where either full of ads or paid so i took matters into my own hand
